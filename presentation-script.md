### PART 2: THE PROBLEM
**Speaker: Andres Jeronimo Parra (Developer)**

> Thank you, Gabriel.
>
> Let me explain the problem we identified at the Mining Environmental Training Center.
>
> Every day, in our workshops, laboratories, and field practices, we generate different types of waste:
>
> **First:** Chemical waste from laboratories - solvents, expired reagents, and acid-base solutions.
>
> **Second:** Mineral samples containing heavy metals like lead, mercury, and arsenic.
>
> **Third:** Contaminated PPE - personal protective equipment used in mining operations.
>
> **Fourth:** Electronic waste - obsolete lab equipment, batteries, and components.
>
> The current problem is that **this waste is not properly classified or managed**. It's mixed together, stored incorrectly, and disposed of without following safety protocols.
>
> This creates serious risks for:
> - **Health** of trainees and staff - respiratory and skin diseases
> - **The environment** - soil and water contamination
> - **Legal compliance** - we're not following current regulations

---

### PART 3: THE SOLUTION
**Speaker: Diego Noriega Vega (UX Designer)**

> Thank you, Andres.
>
> Our solution is a **web platform that simplifies waste management** through a simple, intuitive interface.
>
> The system has **three main features:**
>
> **Feature 1: Waste Registration and Classification**
> Users can quickly register waste and categorize it into four types:
> - 🔴 **Hazardous** (Red container) - chemicals, batteries, contaminated PPE
> - 🔵 **Recyclable** (Blue container) - paper, clean plastics, metals
> - 🟢 **Organic** (Green container) - food waste, garden debris
> - ⚪ **Ordinary** (Gray container) - non-recyclable materials
>
> **Feature 2: Real-Time Reports and Tracking**
> You can view updated data to make better decisions about waste management.
>
> **Feature 3: Alerts and Regulatory Recommendations**
> The system sends alerts and suggestions to ensure we comply with current regulations.
>
> The interface was designed to be **simple, fast, and accessible** for all users - trainees, instructors, and administrative staff.

---

### PART 4: HOW IT WORKS
**Speaker: Samuel Montoya (Quality Analyst)**

> Thank you, Diego.
>
> Let me explain how easy it is to use our system. It works in **three simple steps:**
>
> **Step 1: Select the waste type**
> On the platform, you choose the type of waste you're registering from the dropdown menu.
>
> **Step 2: Register quantity and location**
> Enter how much waste you have and where it's located - workshop, lab, or field area.
>
> **Step 3: Submit for tracking and management**
> Click submit, and the system automatically tracks the waste and notifies the appropriate team for collection.
>
> As a Quality Analyst, I can confirm that the system has been tested to ensure:
> - ✅ All features work correctly
> - ✅ Data is accurate and reliable
> - ✅ The system is fast and responsive
> - ✅ We meet all regulatory requirements
