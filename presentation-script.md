### PART 2 — The Problem
**Speaker: Andres Parra (Developer)**

> Every day, our workshops and labs produce different kinds of waste: chemicals, mineral samples, used protective gear, and old electronics.
>
> Right now, this waste is not sorted correctly. It gets mixed and stored the wrong way.
>
> This puts people's health at risk, harms the environment, and breaks safety rules.

---

### PART 3 — The Solution
**Speaker: Diego Noriega (UX Designer)**

> Our solution is a simple web platform for waste management.
>
> It has three key features:
>
> One, **waste registration**. Users sort waste into four types: hazardous, recyclable, organic, and ordinary.
>
> Two, **live reports**. Everyone can see updated data.
>
> Three, **smart alerts**. The system reminds users to follow safety rules.
>
> It's fast, simple, and easy for everyone to use.

---

### PART 4 — How It Works
**Speaker: Samuel Montoya (Quality Analyst)**

> Using the system takes just three steps.
>
> Step one: select the waste type.
>
> Step two: enter the amount and the location.
>
> Step three: submit the form. The system takes care of the rest.
>
> We tested it fully. It's accurate, fast, and reliable.

---

### PART 5 — Key Benefits
**Speaker: Gabriel Correa (Project Manager)**

> This system gives us five key benefits.
>
> It protects the environment. It keeps us within the law. It builds a culture of care. It saves time and money. And it protects everyone's health.

---

### PART 6 — Conclusion
**Speaker: Andres Parra (Developer)**

> In summary, our system solves a real problem at our training center.
>
> It makes waste easier to sort, track, and manage safely.
>
> Every small action helps. Thank you for listening. We're ready for your questions.
